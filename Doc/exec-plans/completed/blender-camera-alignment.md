# Blender Live Link camera alignment

## Purpose
Match Blender camera viewing direction, screen orientation and projection in the engine, including reopening saved scenes.

## Scope / Non-goals
Camera-local view basis, frame-derived vertical FOV/orthographic width, lens offset, aspect preservation and preview sizing.
Do not change mesh/world conversion, entity hierarchy, native camera defaults or pipeline handedness globally.
Panoramic cameras, render-border cropping, DOF and general sheared camera rigs are out of scope.

## Architecture constraints
Main-thread plugin application; Camera owns view/projection. Optional document fields preserve old asset compatibility.
Persist parameters in scene and prefab paths. Preserve pre-existing dirty changes. Use normal AHT generation.

## Relevant files
Camera.h/.cpp, AssetDocument.h, EntitySerializer, AssetHandlers, PrefabSystem, Scene::UpdateCameras,
LiveLinkSession, camera_sync.py, RenderView preview and numeric smoke fixtures.

## User acceptance
1. Restart Blender and run the rebuilt Debug Editor. Connect and perform one Full Sync to update cameras in existing imported scenes.
2. Select the same camera in Blender and in the engine camera preview; compare the camera render frame, not the free SceneView viewpoint.
3. Compare view direction, roll, screen-left/right and frame coverage; check a parented camera too.
4. Test Sensor Fit AUTO/HORIZONTAL/VERTICAL, portrait frames, Shift X/Y and an orthographic camera.
5. Change render resolution or pixel aspect while connected; the camera preview should retain the source frame and update incrementally.
6. Save both .blend and engine scene; reopen the engine scene without Blender and verify camera framing is retained.

## Remaining limits
Panoramic projections, cropped render borders, depth of field and general sheared camera rigs are not implemented by this change.
Numeric projection/serialization evidence is not a substitute for GPU/image acceptance. Existing native camera defaults are preserved.
